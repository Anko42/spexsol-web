# Discovery call (45–60 min)

Goal: leave the call with a draft of the month-one list and a price the client has already heard. The proposal you send afterwards should contain nothing new.

Timing: agenda 2 min · deep dive 25 min · scope shaping 10 min · price 10 min · close 5 min.

---

## 0. Before the call

- [ ] Re-read the intro-call note. Their pain, their number, their decider, their date.
- [ ] Write a hypothesis of the month-one list: three to five items. You will correct it on the call, but walking in with a draft keeps the call structured.
- [ ] Decide which model you expect to propose: monthly or fixed. Decide the number you will say.
- [ ] Ask in advance for screen share of the real thing (spreadsheet, prototype, backlog). People show more than they tell.

---

## 1. Agenda (2 min)

> SK: „Ďakujem za čas. Navrhujem to takto: najprv mi ukážete, ako to dnes funguje, a budem sa pýtať. Potom spolu spíšeme, čo by bolo v prvom mesiaci a čo určite nie. Na konci vám poviem cenu a dohodneme sa, čo ďalej. Budem si písať poznámky, tie vám potom pošlem. Vyhovuje?“
>
> EN: "Thanks for the time. Here's what I suggest: first you show me how it works today and I ask questions. Then we write down together what month one would contain and what definitely won't. At the end I give you a price and we agree on what's next. I'll take notes and send them to you afterwards. OK?"

---

## 2. Deep dive (25 min)

Start with "show me", then pick the question bank for their persona. Do not ask all of them. Ask until you can describe their day better than they can.

> SK: „Môžete mi to nazdieľať a prejsť to tak, ako to robíte v bežný utorok?“
>
> EN: "Can you share your screen and walk me through it the way you'd do it on a normal Tuesday?"

### Owners and operations (spreadsheet, email, paper)

1. SK: „Kto všetko do toho zapisuje? A kto z toho číta?“ · EN: "Who writes into this? And who reads from it?"
2. SK: „Čo sa stalo naposledy, keď sa tam niečo pokazilo?“ · EN: "What happened the last time something broke in it?"
3. SK: „Ktorá časť vás najviac štve? Nie najdôležitejšia, najotravnejšia.“ · EN: "Which part annoys you most? Not the most important one, the most irritating."
4. SK: „Kam z toho idú dáta ďalej? Faktúry, účtovník, sklad?“ · EN: "Where does the data go from here? Invoices, accountant, warehouse?"
5. SK: „Keby zajtra odišiel [osoba, ktorá tomu rozumie], čo by sa stalo?“ · EN: "If [the person who understands it] left tomorrow, what would happen?"
6. SK: „Skúšali ste už niečo? Hotový softvér, iný dodávateľ? Prečo to nezostalo?“ · EN: "Have you tried anything before? Off-the-shelf software, another vendor? Why didn't it stick?"
7. SK: „Kto by to používal každý deň a ako je na tom s počítačom?“ · EN: "Who would use this every day, and how comfortable are they with computers?"
8. SK: „Čo by sa muselo stať, aby ste o rok povedali, že sa to oplatilo?“ · EN: "What would have to be true a year from now for you to say it was worth it?"

### Founders and new products

1. SK: „Kto je prvý zákazník, ktorý za to zaplatí? Konkrétny človek alebo firma.“ · EN: "Who's the first customer that will pay for this? A specific person or company."
2. SK: „Čo musí vedieť prvá verzia, aby ju ten človek začal používať? A čo môže počkať?“ · EN: "What does the first version need to do for that person to start using it? And what can wait?"
3. SK: „Máte niečo rozrobené? Prototyp, Figmu, Lovable? Ukážte mi to.“ · EN: "Do you have anything started? Prototype, Figma, Lovable? Show me."
4. SK: „Prečo mobil a nie web? Alebo naopak?“ · EN: "Why mobile and not web? Or the other way round?"
5. SK: „Ako budete vedieť, že to funguje? Čo budete merať?“ · EN: "How will you know it's working? What will you measure?"
6. SK: „Kto to bude udržiavať, keď to beží? Vy, ja, niekto tretí?“ · EN: "Who maintains it once it's live? You, me, someone else?"
7. SK: „Čo sa stane s vašimi plánmi, ak to nebude do [ich dátum]?“ · EN: "What happens to your plans if it's not ready by [their date]?"
8. SK: „Koľko ste ochotný do prvej verzie dať, kým uvidíte, či to zákazníci chcú?“ · EN: "How much are you willing to put into the first version before you see whether customers want it?"

### Product teams and CTOs

1. SK: „Ktoré tri veci z backlogu by ste chceli mať hotové do konca kvartálu?“ · EN: "Which three things from the backlog do you want done by end of quarter?"
2. SK: „Prečo ešte nie sú? Kapacita, priority, nikto sa do toho nechce pustiť?“ · EN: "Why aren't they done yet? Capacity, priorities, nobody wants to touch it?"
3. SK: „Ako u vás vyzerá cesta od PR po produkciu? Review, testy, CI?“ · EN: "What's the path from PR to production here? Review, tests, CI?"
4. SK: „Kto by bol môj kontakt na dennej báze a koľko času mi vie dať?“ · EN: "Who'd be my day-to-day contact, and how much time can they give me?"
5. SK: „Aký je stack a je niečo, čoho sa mám báť?“ · EN: "What's the stack, and is there anything I should be scared of?"
6. SK: „Používate už AI nástroje pri vývoji? Máte na to pravidlá?“ · EN: "Do you already use AI tools in development? Any rules around it?"
7. SK: „Ako budeme vedieť, že som sa oplatil? Čo bude iné o tri mesiace?“ · EN: "How will we know I was worth it? What's different in three months?"
8. SK: „Mali ste už externého vývojára? Ako to dopadlo?“ · EN: "Have you had an external developer before? How did it go?"

## 3. Scope shaping (10 min)

Build the written list live, on screen, in a shared doc or plain text. Three columns: **month one**, **later**, **not this**.

> SK: „Poďme to spísať. Čo musí byť hotové na konci prvého mesiaca, aby ste povedali, že to malo zmysel? Hovoríme o troch až piatich veciach, nie o dvadsiatich.“
>
> EN: "Let's write it down. What has to be done at the end of month one for you to say it was worth it? We're talking three to five things, not twenty."

For each item, add how they will check it:

> SK: „Ako si overíte, že je to hotové? Čo si kliknete?“
>
> EN: "How will you check it's done? What will you click on?"

Push things out explicitly. "Later" is a gift, not a rejection.

> SK: „Toto dám do ‚neskôr‘. Nie preto, že to nie je dôležité, ale preto, že bez [prvá položka] to nemá na čom stáť.“
>
> EN: "I'll put this under 'later'. Not because it isn't important, but because without [first item] it has nothing to stand on."

### Monthly or fixed

> SK: „Keď sa na ten zoznam pozriem, zmení sa podľa mňa po dvoch týždňoch, lebo [dôvod]. Preto navrhujem mesačnú spoluprácu: zoznam si meníte, ja dodávam každý týždeň.“
>
> EN: "Looking at this list, I think it'll change after two weeks because [reason]. That's why I suggest the monthly partnership: you change the list, I deliver every week."

> SK: „Tento rozsah je uzavretý a jasný. Dám vám naň pevnú cenu a termín.“
>
> EN: "This scope is closed and clear. I'll give you a fixed price and a date for it."

### It is bigger than one stream

> SK: „Toto sú vlastne dve veci naraz: [A] a [B]. Jeden človek ich poctivo neurobí naraz. Buď vyberieme jednu na prvý mesiac, alebo to naplánujeme za sebou.“
>
> EN: "This is really two things at once: [A] and [B]. One person can't do both properly at the same time. Either we pick one for month one, or we plan them one after the other."

### It is not a fit

> SK: „Toto by som vám nerobil ja. [Jedna veta prečo: natívny Android, hra, ERP migrácia.] Kazil by som vám to a bral za to peniaze.“
>
> EN: "I wouldn't be the right person for this. [One sentence why: native Android, a game, an ERP migration.] I'd do it badly and charge you for it."

## 3b. The MVP conversation (founders)

Use this instead of the generic scope shaping when the client is a founder with a new product. Same three columns, different vocabulary.

### What an MVP is

> SK: „MVP je prvá verzia, za ktorú vám skutočný zákazník zaplatí alebo ju používa každý deň. Nie demo, ktoré ukazujete investorom.“
>
> EN: "An MVP is the first version a real customer pays for or uses every day. Not a demo you show investors."

### Cutting the wish list

Twenty features become three to five. Ask these in order, write the answers into the columns.

> SK: „Na čo klikne váš prvý platiaci zákazník v prvý deň? To je prvý mesiac. Všetko ostatné je zatiaľ ‚neskôr‘.“
>
> EN: "What does your first paying customer click on day one? That's month one. Everything else is 'later' for now."

> SK: „Čo môže byť pre prvých desať zákazníkov manuálny proces v pozadí? Vy to spravíte ručne, oni to nevidia.“
>
> EN: "What can be a manual process behind the scenes for the first ten customers? You do it by hand, they never see it."

> SK: „Čo môže byť namiesto obrazovky e-mail alebo tabuľka? Každá obrazovka, ktorú nepostavíme, je týždeň, ktorý ušetríte.“
>
> EN: "What can be an email or a spreadsheet instead of a screen? Every screen we don't build is a week you save."

### Web first or iOS first

> SK: „Ak sú vaši zákazníci bežní ľudia s telefónom v ruke a potrebujete notifikácie, kameru alebo niečo, čo funguje offline, ide to do App Store. Ak sú to firmy alebo ľudia za počítačom, stačí webová aplikácia, prípadne PWA, a ušetríte si schvaľovanie Apple. Robím web a natívny iOS, natívny Android nie.“
>
> EN: "If your customers are consumers with a phone in their hand and you need push notifications, the camera or something that works offline, it goes to the App Store. If they're businesses or people at a desk, a web app or a PWA is enough and you skip Apple's review. I do web and native iOS, not native Android."

> SK: „Keď si nie ste istý, začnite webom. Z webu sa dá ísť na iOS, keď viete, čo ľudia používajú. Opačne je to drahšie.“
>
> EN: "If you're not sure, start with web. You can go from web to iOS once you know what people use. The other way round is more expensive."

### They have a prototype (Lovable, Cursor, Bolt, Figma)

Ask to see it running, not screenshots. Check four things while they click: how login works, what the data model looks like, whether payments are real or mocked, where it is hosted and who owns the account.

> SK: „Ukážte mi to naživo, nie screenshoty. Pozriem sa na štyri veci: prihlásenie, ako sú uložené dáta, či platby skutočne fungujú a kde to beží.“
>
> EN: "Show me it running, not screenshots. I'll look at four things: login, how the data is stored, whether payments actually work, and where it's hosted."

> SK: „Nechávame, čo funguje. Prerábame iba to, čo neprežije skutočných používateľov. Prototyp nie je stratený čas, je to najlepšie zadanie, aké ste mi mohli dať.“
>
> EN: "We keep what works. We rebuild only what won't survive real users. The prototype isn't wasted time, it's the best brief you could have given me."

### Timeline

> SK: „Typické MVP s troma až piatimi položkami trvá štyri až osem týždňov. Jeden inžinier s AI agentmi, každý týždeň vidíte hotovú časť. Presnejšie vám to poviem, keď máme zoznam uzavretý.“
>
> EN: "A typical MVP with three to five items takes four to eight weeks. One engineer with AI agents, you see a finished piece every week. I'll be more precise once the list is closed."

**Can it be two weeks?**

> SK: „Za dva týždne viem postaviť jednu vec, ktorá funguje od začiatku do konca, nie tri až päť. Ak je to pre vás dosť na prvého zákazníka, poďme do toho. Ak nie, dva týždne nie je MVP, je to demo.“
>
> EN: "In two weeks I can build one thing that works end to end, not three to five. If that's enough for your first customer, let's do it. If not, two weeks isn't an MVP, it's a demo."

### Pricing an MVP

Closed list → fixed price. Founder still learning what to build → fixed-price first slice, then monthly if it goes well (see [capacity.md](capacity.md)). Tie the number to the cost of finding out, not to the features.

> SK: „Zoznam je uzavretý, tak vám dám pevnú cenu a termín. Ak sa počas práce ukáže, že zákazníci chcú niečo iné, zmeníme zoznam a dohodneme sa znova. Neplatíte za funkcie. Platíte za to, aby ste zistili, či to zákazníci chcú, a za to, že to zistíte za pár týždňov namiesto pol roka.“
>
> EN: "The list is closed, so I'll give you a fixed price and a date. If customers turn out to want something else along the way, we change the list and agree again. You're not paying for features. You're paying to find out whether customers want this, and to find out in a few weeks instead of six months."

> SK: „Ešte sami neviete, čo presne bude vo verzii jeden. To je v poriadku. Vtedy je lepšia mesačná spolupráca: každý týždeň niečo pustíme von, vy sa pozriete, čo ľudia robia, a zoznam na ďalší týždeň si upravíte.“
>
> EN: "You don't yet know exactly what version one will contain. That's fine. In that case monthly is better: every week we ship something, you watch what people do, and you adjust the list for the next week."

### Founder red flags

**No first customer named.**

> SK: „Kým nemáte meno človeka, ktorý to bude používať, staviame naslepo. Nájdite mu meno, hoci aj kamaráta, a potom sa ozvite. Rád začnem.“
>
> EN: "Until you have the name of a person who'll use it, we're building blind. Give it a name, even if it's a friend, and then call me. I'll be glad to start."

**Equity instead of cash.**

> SK: „Za podiel nerobím. Nie preto, že neverím vášmu nápadu, ale preto, že by som potom bol spoluzakladateľ a nie dodávateľ, a to je iný vzťah. Ak je rozpočet malý, zmenšíme zoznam.“
>
> EN: "I don't work for equity. Not because I don't believe in your idea, but because that would make me a co-founder, not a vendor, and that's a different relationship. If the budget is small, we shrink the list."

**Wants every feature before launch.**

> SK: „Ak musí byť pred spustením hotové všetko, nestaviame MVP, staviame produkt bez zákazníkov. Vyberme jednu vec, ktorú pustíme von za pár týždňov, a zvyšok necháme rozhodnúť ľudí, ktorí to budú používať.“
>
> EN: "If everything has to be done before launch, we're not building an MVP, we're building a product with no customers. Let's pick one thing to ship in a few weeks and let the people who use it decide the rest."

## 4. Price (10 min)

Structure: recap pain and cost → model → number → silence. Never skip the recap.

> SK: „Zhrniem to. Dnes vás [problém] stojí [ich číslo] mesačne a o pol roka [dôsledok, ktorý povedali]. V prvom mesiaci by sme spravili [položky zo zoznamu]. Navrhujem mesačné partnerstvo za [číslo] eur mesačne bez DPH. Každý týždeň vidíte hotové zmeny, zoznam si meníte, pozastaviť alebo skončiť môžete v ktoromkoľvek mesiaci. Všetko je na vaše meno.“
>
> EN: "Let me sum up. Today [problem] costs you [their number] a month, and in six months [the consequence they named]. In month one we'd do [items from the list]. I propose a monthly partnership at [number] euros a month ex VAT. Every week you see finished changes, you change the list, and you can pause or stop in any month. Everything is in your name."

Then be quiet. Count to five in your head. The first person to talk after the number usually loses.

### Objections

**Too expensive.**

> SK: „Rozumiem. Porovnajme to s tým, čo máte dnes: [ich číslo] mesačne plus riziko, že [dôsledok]. Ak je rozpočet pevný, viem zmenšiť zoznam, nie sadzbu. Čo z prvého mesiaca by ste dali preč?“
>
> EN: "I understand. Let's compare it with what you have today: [their number] a month plus the risk that [consequence]. If the budget is fixed, I can shrink the list, not the rate. What would you drop from month one?"

**We'd prefer hourly.**

> SK: „Hodinovka vás núti strážiť moje hodiny namiesto výsledku. Pri mesačnej cene máte jasno v nákladoch a ja mám motiváciu robiť veci rýchlo, nie dlho. Ak je pre vás hodinovka nutnosť, nebudem ten správny.“
>
> EN: "Hourly makes you watch my hours instead of the result. With a monthly price you know your cost, and I'm motivated to be fast, not slow. If hourly is a must for you, I'm not the right fit."

**We want a fixed price but the scope isn't clear.**

> SK: „Pevnú cenu dám na uzavretý zoznam. Toto ešte uzavreté nie je. Buď spravíme týždenný audit za [500–1 500] eur a z neho vznikne zoznam, na ktorý dám cenu, alebo začneme mesačne a cenu za zvyšok spresníme po prvom mesiaci.“
>
> EN: "I give a fixed price for a closed list. This one isn't closed yet. Either we do a one-week audit for [500–1 500] euros and I price the list that comes out of it, or we start monthly and I firm up the rest after month one."

**My nephew / an agency abroad can do it cheaper.**

> SK: „Môže. Rozdiel je v tom, kto za to ručí o rok, keď to treba zmeniť. Ak vám stačí lacnejšia verzia, nebudem vás prehovárať. Ak ju budete potrebovať zachraňovať, ozvite sa.“
>
> EN: "They might. The difference is who stands behind it a year from now when it needs changing. If a cheaper version is enough for you, I won't talk you out of it. If it ever needs rescuing, call me."

**What if you get hit by a bus?**

> SK: „Všetko je vo vašom repozitári od prvého dňa: kód, dokumentácia, dôvody rozhodnutí. Zadanie je spísané, zmeny majú testy. Ktorýkoľvek skúsený vývojár to prevezme. To je aj dôvod, prečo je všetko na vaše meno a nie moje.“
>
> EN: "Everything is in your repository from day one: code, documentation, the reasoning behind decisions. The brief is written down, changes have tests. Any experienced developer can take it over. That's also why everything is in your name, not mine."

**AI-written code, is it any good?**

> SK: „AI píše rutinu, ja rozhodujem o architektúre a kontrolujem každú zmenu pred nasadením. Výsledok posudzujte podľa toho, čo každý týždeň uvidíte, nie podľa toho, kto napísal riadok. Ak chcete, prvý týždeň si kód pozrie váš človek.“
>
> EN: "AI writes the routine parts, I own the architecture and review every change before it ships. Judge the result by what you see each week, not by who typed the line. If you like, your own person can review the code in week one."

**We need it in two weeks.**

> SK: „Za dva týždne viem dodať [najmenšia použiteľná časť], nie celý zoznam. Ak je ten termín pevný, vyberme teraz, čo musí byť v ňom, a zvyšok ide po ňom.“
>
> EN: "In two weeks I can deliver [the smallest usable part], not the whole list. If that date is fixed, let's pick now what has to be in it, and the rest comes after."

## 5. Saying no (when it has to happen)

Say it on the call, not in an email three days later.

**Bad fit (wrong work).**

> SK: „Toto nie je moja parketa a nechcem vám to robiť na polovicu. Pošlem vám kontakt na [typ dodávateľa].“
>
> EN: "This isn't my field and I don't want to do it half-well for you. I'll send you a contact for [type of vendor]."

**No budget.**

> SK: „Pri tomto rozpočte by som vám nedodal to, čo potrebujete, a nechcem vám sľubovať niečo iné. Keď sa to zmení, rád sa ozvem. Medzitým: [jedna konkrétna rada zadarmo].“
>
> EN: "At this budget I wouldn't deliver what you need, and I don't want to promise something else. When that changes, I'm happy to talk. In the meantime: [one concrete free tip]."

**Wrong tech / wrong platform.**

> SK: „Natívny Android ani [X] nerobím. Robím web a iOS. Ak je Android nutnosť, radšej vám poviem rovno, než aby ste to zistili o tri mesiace.“
>
> EN: "I don't do native Android or [X]. I do web and iOS. If Android is a must, I'd rather tell you now than have you find out in three months."

Referral line, always:

> SK: „Ak poznáte niekoho, kto rieši [typ problému, ktorý robíte], budem vďačný za odporúčanie.“
>
> EN: "If you know anyone dealing with [the type of problem you solve], I'd be grateful for the referral."

---

## 6. Close (5 min)

> SK: „Takže: pošlem vám do zajtra [čas] jednostranovú ponuku s tým, čo sme si teraz spísali, a cenou, ktorú ste počuli. Nič nové tam nebude. Potrebujete ešte niekoho, aby ju videl? Kedy mi viete dať odpoveď? Ak áno, začať viem [dátum].“
>
> EN: "So: by tomorrow [time] I'll send you a one-page proposal with what we just wrote down and the price you've heard. Nothing new in it. Does anyone else need to see it? When can you give me an answer? If it's a yes, I can start on [date]."

Book the answer date in your calendar before you hang up.

### Proposal skeleton (one page)

```
Goal (their words)
Month one (3–5 items, each with "how you'll check it")
Later (explicitly out of month one)
Model: Monthly partnership | Fixed-price project
Price: ... € / month ex VAT   |   ... € ex VAT
How we work: weekly delivery, your repo and accounts, pause or stop any month
Start date:
Valid until:
```

## 7. Follow-up sequence

**Day 0, recap (same day).**

> SK:
> Predmet: Ponuka a zoznam z dnešného hovoru
>
> Dobrý deň [meno],
> v prílohe je ponuka. Je v nej presne to, čo sme si dnes spísali, a cena, ktorú ste počuli: [číslo] eur mesačne bez DPH.
> Ak súhlasíte, viem začať [dátum]. Odpoveď potrebujem do [dátum], aby som vám ten termín držal.
>
> Andrej

> EN:
> Subject: Proposal and list from today's call
>
> Hi [name],
> the proposal is attached. It contains exactly what we wrote down today and the price you heard: [number] euros a month ex VAT.
> If you agree, I can start on [date]. I need an answer by [date] to hold that slot.
>
> Andrej

**Day 3, nudge.**

> SK: „Dobrý deň [meno], len sa pripomínam k ponuke z [deň]. Je niečo, čo vám v nej chýba alebo čo by som mal upraviť?“
>
> EN: "Hi [name], just following up on the proposal from [day]. Is there anything missing or anything I should adjust?"

**Day 10, last touch.**

> SK: „Dobrý deň [meno], predpokladám, že teraz nie je správny čas. Termín [dátum] uvoľním. Ak sa to zmení, stačí napísať, rád sa k tomu vrátim.“
>
> EN: "Hi [name], I assume now isn't the right time. I'll release the [date] slot. If that changes, just write, I'm happy to pick it up again."

After day 10, stop. Add them to a quarterly check-in list and move on.

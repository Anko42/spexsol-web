# Intro call (15 min)

Goal: find out in 15 minutes whether you can help, and book the next step. You are not selling on this call. You are qualifying.

Timing: opening 1 min · questions 8 min · pitch 1 min · price 2 min · close 2 min · buffer 1 min.

---

## 0. Before the call (2 min)

- [ ] Open their website, app or LinkedIn. Write down **one specific observation** (an old app update date, a manual process visible on the site, a job ad for an admin role, a recent post).
- [ ] Note which persona they are: owner with a spreadsheet, founder with an idea, team with a backlog.
- [ ] Open your calendar so you can book the next call live.
- [ ] Have this file open. Have the note template from section 7 ready.

---

## 1. Opening (1 min)

Set the frame first. It kills awkward small talk and tells them you respect their time.

> SK: „Dobrý deň, ďakujem za čas. Mám pre nás 15 minút. Cieľ je jednoduchý: zistiť, či vám viem pomôcť. Ak nie, poviem vám to rovno a možno vám poradím niekoho iného. Môžem?“
>
> EN: "Hi, thanks for taking the time. We have 15 minutes. The goal is simple: find out whether I can help. If I can't, I'll tell you straight and maybe point you to someone who can. Sound good?"

Then your one observation. This is the small talk. One sentence, one question, then move on.

> SK: „Videl som, že [pozorovanie]. Je to ešte aktuálne?“
>
> EN: "I noticed that [observation]. Is that still the case?"

If they start a long story, let them finish one point, then:

> SK: „Super, k tomu sa ešte dostaneme. Môžem sa najprv opýtať pár vecí?“
>
> EN: "Great, we'll come back to that. Can I ask you a few things first?"

---

## 2. Three qualifying questions (8 min)

Ask in this order. Write down their exact words. Their words go into the proposal later.

### Q1. What do you do today, and what does it cost you?

> SK: „Ako to riešite dnes? Kto to robí, ako často a koľko času mu to berie?“
>
> EN: "How do you handle it today? Who does it, how often, and how much of their time does it take?"

Follow-ups until you have a number (hours per week, people, money, lost orders):

> SK: „Keď to prepočítame na týždeň, koľko hodín to je?“ / „Čo sa stane, keď sa pomýli?“
>
> EN: "If we add that up per week, how many hours is it?" / "What happens when someone makes a mistake?"

### Q2. What happens if nothing changes in six months?

> SK: „Keby ste to nechali tak, ako to je, čo bude o pol roka?“
>
> EN: "If you left it as it is, where would you be in six months?"

Listen for urgency. "Nothing much" means low urgency; go to the polite no. "We can't hire another admin" or "we lose the client" means real urgency.

### Q3. Who decides, and when do you want this live?

> SK: „Kto o tom u vás rozhoduje okrem vás? A kedy by to malo bežať?“
>
> EN: "Who else is involved in the decision? And when do you want it running?"

> SK: „Má to už nejaký rozpočet, alebo sa to bude schvaľovať?“
>
> EN: "Is there a budget for this already, or does it still need approval?"

### Listening cues

Green flags:
- They quantify the pain without you pushing.
- The person on the call is the decider or sits next to them.
- They have a date or event driving the timeline.
- They ask how you work, not just how much you cost.

Red flags:
- "We just want a quote to compare with others." (Price shopper. Offer the audit or decline.)
- "Can you bill hourly?" (They want control over hours, not outcomes. Explain the model once; if they insist, decline.)
- "We're talking to six agencies." (You will not win on price. Qualify harder or decline.)
- Nobody knows who approves the budget.
- "Can you build it in two weeks for free as a test?"

---

## 3. Pitch in 60 seconds (1 min)

Only after Q1–Q3. Use their words from Q1. Keep it to four sentences.

> SK: „Takto fungujem ja. Som jeden inžinier, ktorý používa AI agentov, takže sa hýbem rýchlo, ale za každú zmenu ručím osobne. Každý týždeň vidíte hotovú prácu, nie reporty. Všetko, čo vznikne, repozitár aj účty, je od prvého dňa na vaše meno. Spolupracujeme buď mesačne, podľa zoznamu priorít, ktorý si môžete meniť, alebo na pevnú cenu, keď je zadanie uzavreté.“
>
> EN: "Here's how I work. I'm one engineer using AI agents, so I move fast, but I personally stand behind every change. Every week you see finished work, not status reports. Everything that gets built, the repo and the accounts, is in your name from day one. We work either monthly, against a priority list you can change, or at a fixed price when the scope is closed."

Then tie it back:

> SK: „Vo vašom prípade by som začal tým, čo ste spomínali: [ich slová z Q1].“
>
> EN: "In your case I'd start with what you mentioned: [their words from Q1]."

---

## 4. Price talk (2 min)

Rule: no number before pain. If they ask early, deflect once with a range and a condition.

### They ask "how much?" in minute 3

> SK: „Poviem vám rozsah, ale najprv potrebujem vedieť, čo to má riešiť, inak by som strieľal naslepo. Mesačná spolupráca sa u mňa zvyčajne pohybuje v nižších tisícoch eur mesačne. Či je to pre vás zmysluplné, závisí od toho, koľko vás dnešný stav stojí. Môžem sa opýtať na to najprv?“
>
> EN: "I'll give you a range, but first I need to know what it has to solve, otherwise I'm guessing. Monthly work with me usually lands in the low thousands of euros per month. Whether that makes sense for you depends on what the current setup costs you. Can I ask about that first?"

### End of call, after Q1–Q3

Anchor against their number from Q1 before you say yours.

> SK: „Povedali ste, že vás to dnes stojí zhruba [X hodín / Y eur] mesačne. Mesačná spolupráca je u mňa od 2 500 do 3 500 eur bez DPH, podľa toho, koľko z môjho týždňa to zaberie. Ak chcete najprv menší krok, urobím platený audit za 500 až 1 500 eur: týždeň, na konci máte písomný zoznam, čo treba postaviť a v akom poradí. Ten zoznam je potom prvý mesiac spolupráce, alebo si ho vezmete a postavíte to s kýmkoľvek.“
>
> EN: "You said this costs you roughly [X hours / Y euros] a month today. A monthly partnership with me is 2 500 to 3 500 euros ex VAT, depending on how much of my week it takes. If you'd rather start smaller, I do a paid audit for 500 to 1 500 euros: one week, and at the end you have a written list of what to build and in what order. That list becomes month one of our work, or you take it and build it with anyone."

Then stop talking. Let them react. Do not fill the silence with a discount.

### Fixed-price request

> SK: „Pevnú cenu viem dať, keď je zadanie uzavreté. Ak ešte nie je, prvý krok je audit, z neho vznikne zoznam a na ten dám cenu.“
>
> EN: "I can give a fixed price once the scope is closed. If it isn't yet, the first step is the audit, that produces the list, and I price the list."

---

## 5. Close (2 min)

Pick one of three outcomes. Say the sentence. Book it on the call.

### (a) Discovery call

> SK: „Dáva mi to zmysel. Navrhujem 45-minútový hovor, kde si spolu prejdeme [ich Excel / prototyp / backlog] a spíšeme, čo by bolo v prvom mesiaci. Vyhovuje vám [deň] o [čas]?“
>
> EN: "This makes sense to me. I suggest a 45-minute call where we go through [their spreadsheet / prototype / backlog] together and write down what month one would contain. Does [day] at [time] work?"

### (b) Paid audit

> SK: „Najčistejší prvý krok je audit. Pošlem vám dnes jednostranovú ponuku: rozsah, cena, termín. Ak ju odsúhlasíte do [deň], začnem [deň].“
>
> EN: "The cleanest first step is the audit. I'll send you a one-page offer today: scope, price, date. If you approve it by [day], I start on [day]."

### (c) Polite no + referral

Use when urgency is low, budget is missing, or it is the wrong kind of work.

> SK: „Budem úprimný: toto nie je niečo, kde by som vám dal najlepšiu hodnotu za peniaze. [Dôvod v jednej vete.] Ak chcete, pošlem vám kontakt na niekoho, kto sa tomu venuje. A ak poznáte niekoho, kto rieši [váš typ problému], budem rád za odporúčanie.“
>
> EN: "I'll be honest: this isn't something where I'd give you the best value for money. [One-sentence reason.] If you like, I'll send you a contact who does this. And if you know anyone dealing with [your type of problem], I'd appreciate the referral."

Never end with "I'll send a proposal" and no date.

## 6. After the call (same day)

### Follow-up email

> SK:
> Predmet: Zhrnutie hovoru a ďalší krok
>
> Dobrý deň [meno],
> ďakujem za hovor. Ak som to pochopil správne, dnes vás najviac brzdí [problém ich slovami] a stojí vás to zhruba [číslo].
> Dohodli sme sa na: [ďalší krok s dátumom].
> Ak som niečo zle pochopil, napíšte mi prosím.
>
> Andrej

> EN:
> Subject: Call summary and next step
>
> Hi [name],
> thanks for the call. If I understood correctly, the biggest blocker today is [problem in their words] and it costs you roughly [number].
> We agreed on: [next step with date].
> If I got anything wrong, please tell me.
>
> Andrej

### Note template

Keep one note per lead (a plain text file, a Notion row, anything). Fields:

```
Company / person:
Persona: owner | founder | team
Pain (their words):
Cost today (hours/week, €/month, people):
If nothing changes:
Decider:
Timeline / driver:
Budget known: yes | no | approval needed
Flags: green / red
Next step + date:
```

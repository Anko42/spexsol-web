# Capacity: what to sell while localhost is the main client

Internal only. Read before promising a start date or a monthly partnership.

## Situation

localhost s.r.o. is the base income and expects availability five days a week. Load varies by week. Side work happens in evenings and on weekends, with AI agents doing routine work. Hard cap: 50 hours a week in total. That leaves roughly 10 hours a week, about 5 days a month, for own clients.

## Order of what to sell

1. **Audit** (1–2 weeks, 500–1 500 €). Has an end date. Fits evenings. Converts to the next step on your terms.
2. **Fixed-price project** (MVP or a closed list). Start date you choose, end date exists, no weekly promise.
3. **Monthly partnership**. Only after one fixed project shipped without a missed localhost day, with a client who proved low-touch, and only one at a time. Sell it as roughly 5 days a month at 2 000–2 500 €. Say on the call that finished changes come regularly, not on a fixed weekday.

When a lead asks for monthly first: "Najskôr audit alebo malý projekt, mesačne až keď sa spoznáme."

## Rules that protect localhost

- Nothing for own clients during localhost hours, on localhost hardware, accounts, repos or networks. Agents for own clients run on own machine and own keys.
- No work for localhost customers, partners or end customers, directly or indirectly, for 24 months after the contract ends. Ask every lead where they heard of you and whether they work with localhost. If the answer touches localhost, decline.
- Nothing learned at localhost goes anywhere: no code, no client names, no "we did something similar for".
- Reply to own clients within one business day, in the evening slot. Say so up front so nobody expects daytime answers.
- If localhost asks: "Mám popri vás jedného malého klienta po večeroch, nič z vašej oblasti ani z vašich zákazníkov." Then stop talking.

## Stop signals

Any one of these means pause side work, not localhost:

- two weeks in a row over 50 hours
- a missed localhost deadline
- a client who messages during the day and expects answers
- sleep under six hours for a week

## When to renegotiate localhost

Three consecutive months with own revenue of 2 500 € or more and one happy reference. Then ask localhost for a four-day week. Not before.
